Build the platform, integrate the intelligence.
- Own research state, workflows, evidence and user experience.
- Reuse mature open-source research engines instead of rebuilding them.
- Integrate through stable, typed and versioned contracts.
- Keep core business logic independent of external frameworks.
- Treat every engine, skill and compute backend as replaceable.
- Preserve user control, security, reproducibility and research provenance.
- Prefer a modular monolith over premature microservices.
- Require real integration tests before declaring an adapter production-ready.
